You're set

  • Fullscreen: the expand button on the player. ←/→ change visualizer, L toggles lyrics.
  • Ctrl+Alt+Space plays/pauses from any app. Ctrl+Alt+O shows floating lyrics.
  • The tray icon has the mini player, floating lyrics and a sleep timer.
